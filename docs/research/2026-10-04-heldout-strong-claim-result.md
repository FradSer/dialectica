# Completed strong-model claim-format single control

Fifth of seven frozen studies completed. GPT-5.5 single solves 24/24 trials
(8 held-out portfolio tasks × 3 repeats) under the answer-plus-five-claims
contract. No generation failures or unknown calls were observed. Reported cost
is 48,669 tokens / 24 observable turns, mean 2,027.875 tokens per trial.
Terminal report/protocol/analysis byte hashes, task/configuration bindings and
all record-level usage sums were verified against the unchanged frozen campaign.

The separate meta-format single control was also 24/24, at 39,495 tokens.
Together they show an observed ceiling for GPT on this task pool under both
output contracts. Degenerate bootstrap intervals [1, 1] do not prove certainty
on unseen tasks. Different prompts/calls and reported-token counts do not prove
matched compute or billing. The claim-format contract itself adds output work.

Only strong single arms were declared. This result cannot establish benefits
of claim falsification or staged/direct control on GPT; weak-model mechanism
results and strong-model control results must remain separate. It also does
not establish a universal rule that orchestration cannot help.

The two Gemini meta-controller budget studies and final environment verification
remain. No new stable API promotion follows from these strong controls.

Artifacts: `results/2026-10-04-heldout-v1/strong-claim-single.json`,
`strong-claim-single.protocol.json`, `strong-claim-single.analysis.json` and
`strong-claim-single.terminal.json` in that same directory.
